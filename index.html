CHAPTER 01 · OUR STORY

A little place
to land.

Litterbox Cafe is an imagined neighborhood hideaway for unhurried mornings, comforting cups, and conversations that make an ordinary day feel special.

Inspired by the quiet charm of Baguio and the curious cats who always find the sunniest seat, we made a place that feels easy to come back to.
